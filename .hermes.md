## Intended information hierarchy

Keep the page concise and thumb-friendly. Confirm the exact links with Illan before coding, then prefer:

1. Identity/profile header.
2. One current priority CTA or event.
3. Primary links grouped by real user intent (watch, follow, play/use, work/contact).
4. Compact social controls.
5. Optional stand-up/media preview only if it remains fast and useful.
6. Short footer/contact.

Do not preserve sections merely because the old page has them. Do not add fake metrics, placeholder links, stale events, or dead disabled cards.

## Reusable portfolio components

Adapt only what earns its place:

- Color and border tokens.
- `brand-light` identity indicator.
- `pixel-button` pressed-state language.
- Compact `project-card` shell for priority links.
- `terminal-bar` / `terminal-status` for profile or section framing.
- `section-kicker` typography.
- Sparse scanlines and grid texture.
- At most one or two `float-tile` decorations, never colliding with content.
- Optional SFX only if Illan asks for it; no automatic audio.

Avoid copying the portfolio’s sticky three-column header, giant two-column hero, project-proof panels, or recruiter-focused content architecture.
